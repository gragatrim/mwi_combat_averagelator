The Retry Problem

On floors with <100% clear rate, rooms above your skip threshold are attempted but may fail. Each failed attempt costs 120 seconds (full timeout). On F9 (68% clear rate), you average 0.47 retries per room — nearly doubling the time per room from 38s to 98s. On F13 (30%), you average 2.33 retries per room — each room takes 5+ minutes.

The high gold per room on F11–F13 (518k–681k) can't compensate for the massive retry overhead. Even F12's 681k/room at 261s/room = only 9.4m/h — below your outside income benchmark.

💡 Exception: F2–F7 Exploration (100% Clear Rate)

Floors 2–7 have 100% clear rate — no retries needed. Exploration on these floors earns 12–14m/h, which is above your 10m/h outside income but well below the 24.7m/h rush-only rate. Full clearing these floors costs extra time that could be spent finishing the run faster.

When might F2–F7 exploration make sense?

  • If you're already at F13 with torches to spare and have nothing else to do
  • If you value total gold per run over gold per hour (e.g., saving for a big purchase)
  • If you're token farming (exploration tokens on F2–F7 are decent at 139–162 tok/h)

But for pure gold/h efficiency: rush everything and exit.
