⏱️ Fixed Cooldown Changes Everything

The cooldown is fixed at 56h from entry. Whether you spend 3h or 7h in the lab, your next run starts at the same time. This means every extra minute exploring is a minute you lose from outside income. The optimization isn't "maximize lab gold/h" — it's "maximize total gold per cycle":

cycle_gold = lab_gold + (56h − active_time) × 10m/h

The Automation Limitation

F2–F7 exploration earns 12–14m/h (above 10m/h) — these floors have 100% clear rate, zero retries. F8+ exploration earns 7–9m/h (below 10m/h) — room retries at 120s each destroy the value.

The ideal strategy would be: Full Clear F2–F7, Rush F8–F13. But the game only lets you set a single rush threshold — you can't say "FC low floors, rush high floors." Setting "Rush 1–1, FC 2–13" would force you to full-clear F8–F13 too, wasting time on retry-heavy floors.

So "Rush all" is the best automation setting, even though it leaves the F2–F7 value on the table. If you wanted to manually full-clear F2–F7 while rushing F8+, you'd gain ~6m per cycle. Probably not worth the hassle.
