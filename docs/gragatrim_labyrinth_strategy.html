The F14–F15 Time Cliff

Floors 4–13 average ~30 min each and yield 340–640k gold/min. Then F14 takes 93 minutes (3× longer!) for only 18.9m gold, and F15 takes 114 minutes for 20.6m gold. These two floors alone account for 3.5 hours — 40% of total run time but only 26% of total gold.

The marginal gold/min drops from 342k (F13) → 203k (F14) → 180k (F15) — a 47% drop. This is because clearability plummets to 16% and 13%, meaning you're failing most rooms and spending 90+ seconds on each failed attempt before finding a viable path.
